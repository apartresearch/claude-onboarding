# Starter prompts

Examples to adapt to your own work: paste one into a Code session and change the names. Each says which connectors it uses.

## Start here

**Where my work lives**

> Look through my Slack channels, Drive, calendar and Notion, and summarise my main projects, the channels and folders they live in, and the people I work with. Show it to me before saving anything.

Slack, Drive, Calendar, Notion.

## Every day

**Morning briefing** (then ask Claude to run it every weekday)

> Tell me what needs me today: unanswered emails and Slack messages, today's meetings and what to prepare, my Notion tasks due this week.

Gmail, Slack, Calendar, Notion.

**Ball in my court**

> Find every partner or advisor conversation from the last 6 weeks where I owe the next reply, and draft each follow-up in my Gmail drafts.

Gmail, Slack.

**Meeting to tasks**

> Turn today's call with [person] into tasks in my Notion project, with owners and due dates, and draft a 5-line summary for #[channel].

Gmail or Drive (Gemini notes), Notion, Slack.

## Sprints

**Check-in update**

> Draft my update for the sprint check-in: where my sprint stands against its date, what's blocked and on whom, and what I'll finish before next week. Use my Notion sprint project, my Slack channels and my last week of email.

Notion, Slack, Gmail.

**Judges and speakers**

> Find 30 researchers who'd make strong judges for a [topic] hackathon, with recent work, why they fit and how to reach them, in a new Google Sheet. Give a source link for every row.

Web, Drive.

**Where to announce**

> Find 20 communities whose members would want the [hackathon] sprint, and draft a post for each that fits how that community announces things.

Web.

## Partners, funders and growth

**Relationship tracker**

> Build a Notion database of everyone I've talked to about [programme], from email, Slack and meeting notes, with organisation, last contact, status and next step.

Gmail, Slack, Drive, Notion.

**Outside inquiries**

> Sort new outside inquiries from the last day into partner, participant, press and spam, and draft replies in my Gmail drafts.

Gmail, Slack.

**A document from what we already have**

> Write the two-pager for funders on [programme] from everything in Drive and Notion, as a new Google Doc.

Drive, Notion.

**Check a document**

> Read [Doc name] and suggest edits for clarity and consistency, as suggestions in the Doc.

Docs.
